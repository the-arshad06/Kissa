# KISSA – A Tale of Untold Stories

> **Preserving the stories that history forgot.**

KISSA is a crowd-sourced digital platform for preserving forgotten local histories, historical places, oral stories, personal memories, and cultural heritage.

Many small historical places and local stories are not well documented on popular platforms. KISSA gives people a place to share these stories and help preserve them for future generations.

---

## 📌 About the Project

KISSA works like a digital notebook where users can document and discover stories that may otherwise be forgotten.

Users can create an account, share stories with photos, explore stories from the community, and interact with other users.

The main idea behind KISSA is to give ordinary people a platform to preserve the history and memories of their local communities.

---

## 🎯 Problem Statement

Many historical places, traditions, local events, personal memories, and oral stories are not documented on popular platforms.

Most well-known historical resources focus on famous monuments, major events, and widely recognized historical figures.

As a result, many small but meaningful local stories can disappear over time.

KISSA aims to solve this problem by providing a community-driven platform where people can document, preserve, and discover these untold stories.

---

## 💡 Proposed Solution

KISSA provides a digital platform where users can:

- Create an account
- Create and share historical stories
- Add photographs to their stories
- Discover stories shared by other users
- Search for stories and users
- Like and comment on stories
- Follow other users
- Share stories with others
- Manage their own profile

This creates a community-driven digital archive of local history and cultural memories.
